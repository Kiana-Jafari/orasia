# Global Trade Gateway

Build a professional, modern, multilingual-ready corporate website for an international B2B trading company.

IMPORTANT:

This is NOT a SaaS company, NOT a technology startup, NOT an e-commerce store, and NOT a personal portfolio.

The company is an international trading business involved in import and export of physical products. The primary purpose of the website is to establish credibility, present the company professionally to international clients and business partners, showcase import and export products, communicate the company's business activities and markets, and provide clear contact information.

The website should feel like a legitimate, established international trading company that a foreign buyer, supplier, distributor, or business partner would feel comfortable contacting.

The design must prioritize:

- Trust

- Professionalism

- International presence

- B2B credibility

- Simplicity

- Premium but restrained visual design

- Clear information hierarchy

- Excellent typography

- High-quality imagery

- Fast loading

- Responsive design

Do NOT make the website look like a generic AI-generated landing page.

Do NOT use:

- Purple/blue SaaS gradients

- Excessive glassmorphism

- Excessive rounded cards

- Huge decorative gradients

- Neon colors

- Overly playful illustrations

- Cartoon graphics

- Excessive animations

- Cryptocurrency/startup aesthetics

- Generic technology imagery

- Fake statistics or fake testimonials

- Fake partner logos

- Fake certifications

- Fake awards

- Fake company history

- Invented business claims

If information is not provided, use clearly marked placeholders instead of inventing facts.

--------------------------------------------------

BRAND DIRECTION

--------------------------------------------------

Create a refined international corporate identity.

Visual character:

- Elegant

- Minimal

- Confident

- Professional

- Trustworthy

- Contemporary

- International

- B2B

- Premium without looking luxurious or extravagant

The website should resemble the visual quality of a serious international trading, sourcing, distribution, commodities, food, or logistics company.

Use a restrained color palette:

- Primary: deep navy / charcoal

- Secondary: warm off-white / ivory

- Neutral: white and light gray

- Accent: subtle muted gold or bronze, used sparingly

Do not make gold dominant.

Typography:

Use a professional modern sans-serif typeface with excellent readability.

Possible font direction:

- Inter

- Manrope

- DM Sans

- or another refined modern sans-serif

Use strong typographic hierarchy rather than decorative elements.

Headings should feel confident and editorial.

Body text should be highly readable.

Use generous whitespace.

SITE STRUCTURE

--------------------------------------------------

Create four main pages:

1. Home

2. About Us

3. Products

4. Contact

The primary navigation should be:

Home

About Us

Products

Contact

Include a clear "Contact Us" CTA in the navigation.

The navigation should be consistent across all pages.

Use a responsive navigation menu on mobile.

--------------------------------------------------

PAGE 1 — HOME

--------------------------------------------------

The homepage should immediately communicate:

- Who the company is

- What the company does

- That it operates internationally

- That it specializes in import/export and trading

- How visitors can explore products or contact the company

SECTION 1 — HERO

Create a large, sophisticated hero section.

Layout:

- Large high-quality background image or split layout

- Strong headline

- Short supporting paragraph

- Two CTA buttons

Suggested placeholder headline:

"Connecting Markets. Delivering Opportunities."

Alternative headline direction:

"International Trade, Built on Trust."

Supporting text:

"[Company Name] is an international trading company engaged in the sourcing, import, export, and distribution of selected products across global markets."

Do not make the paragraph too long.

Primary CTA:

"Explore Products"

Secondary CTA:

"Contact Us"

The hero should visually communicate international commerce, logistics, trade, or high-quality products.

Avoid generic images of people shaking hands in suits.

Prefer authentic-looking imagery related to:

- International trade

- Ports

- Shipping containers

- Cargo

- Seafood / food products if relevant

- Warehouses

- Global logistics

- Product sourcing

- International markets

Use a dark subtle overlay if text is placed over an image.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 2 — SHORT COMPANY INTRODUCTION

--------------------------------------------------

Create a concise "Who We Are" section.

Heading:

"Who We Are"

Include a short 2–3 paragraph introduction.

Use placeholder content where company-specific information is required.

Example:

"[Company Name] is a trading company focused on building reliable connections between suppliers, buyers, and international markets.

Through our sourcing network and commercial partnerships, we facilitate the movement of selected products across borders with a focus on quality, reliability, and long-term business relationships."

Include a "Learn More About Us" link/button leading to the About Us page.

Layout:

- Text on one side

- High-quality relevant image on the other side

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 3 — BUSINESS ACTIVITIES

--------------------------------------------------

Create a section titled:

"What We Do"

Show 3 or 4 business activities.

Possible categories:

01 — Import

Sourcing and importing selected products from international suppliers.

02 — Export

Connecting quality products with international buyers and markets.

03 — Sourcing

Identifying suitable suppliers and products according to market requirements.

04 — Trade & Distribution

Facilitating reliable commercial relationships and product movement.

Keep the descriptions concise.

Do not use excessive card styling.

Use simple editorial blocks or minimal cards.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 4 — FEATURED PRODUCTS

--------------------------------------------------

Create a "Featured Products" section.

Show 3–6 product previews.

Each product should include:

- High-quality product image

- Product name

- Category

- Short description

- Optional "View Product" link

Do not invent actual products.

Use placeholders such as:

"Product Name"

"Product Category"

"Short product description"

Include a CTA:

"View All Products"

leading to the Products page.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 5 — GLOBAL MARKETS

--------------------------------------------------

Create a section communicating the company's international orientation.

Heading:

"Connecting Markets"

Include a short description about international sourcing, trade, and market relationships.

Use a subtle world map or geographic visual if it can be implemented elegantly.

Do NOT use a cheap-looking spinning globe.

If no real countries are provided, use placeholders rather than inventing countries.

Example:

"Markets: [Country / Region], [Country / Region], [Country / Region]"

The visual should communicate international reach without making unsupported claims.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 6 — TRUST / VALUE PROPOSITION

--------------------------------------------------

Create a restrained section explaining why business partners should work with the company.

Heading:

"Built for Long-Term Business"

Possible value points:

- Reliable sourcing

- Quality-focused selection

- Responsive communication

- International market knowledge

- Long-term partnerships

Do not create fake numerical statistics.

Do not claim "20+ years of experience" or similar unless provided.

--------------------------------------------------

SECTION 7 — CONTACT CTA

--------------------------------------------------

End the homepage with a strong but simple CTA.

Heading:

"Let's Work Together"

Supporting text:

"For product inquiries, sourcing requests, or international trade opportunities, get in touch with our team."

Button:

"Contact Us"

Hero:

Title:

"About [Company Name]"

SECTION — COMPANY OVERVIEW

- Geographic focus

SECTION — OUR MISSION

"Our Mission"

SECTION — OUR ACTIVITIES

- Export

SECTION — OUR APPROACH

Quality

SECTION — COMPANY / OFFICE IMAGE

Include an authentic company image placeholder.

Do not generate fake office photographs that imply a real physical office if none has been provided.

--------------------------------------------------

PAGE 3 — PRODUCTS

--------------------------------------------------

Create a professional product catalogue page.

Page heading:

"Our Products"

Supporting text:

"Explore our selected range of products available for international sourcing, import, and export."

Divide the catalogue into two clearly distinguishable sections:

IMPORT PRODUCTS

and

EXPORT PRODUCTS

Use tabs or clear section navigation if appropriate, but keep the UX simple.

Each product card should contain:

- Product image

- Product name

- Product category

- Short description

- Key specifications if available

- "Request Information" CTA

Do not show prices unless explicitly provided.

For B2B products, use language such as:

"Request Information"

"Request a Quote"

"Inquire About This Product"

rather than:

"Buy Now"

"Add to Cart"

This is NOT an e-commerce website.

--------------------------------------------------

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

--------------------------------------------------

The website must be fully responsive.

Optimize for:

Desktop:

1440px

1280px

1024px

Tablet:

768px

Mobile:

480px

390px

375px

On mobile:

- Navigation becomes a clean hamburger menu

- Product grids become one column or two columns depending on screen width

- Typography scales appropriately

- Buttons remain easily tappable

- Images maintain correct aspect ratio

- Avoid horizontal scrolling

- Maintain generous but efficient spacing

The website should look intentionally designed for mobile, not simply compressed from desktop.

--------------------------------------------------

UX REQUIREMENTS

--------------------------------------------------

Keep navigation extremely clear.

The visitor should always know:

- Where they are

- What the company does

- How to view products

- How to contact the company

Use:

- Clear CTAs

- Consistent buttons

- Breadcrumbs where appropriate

- Strong visual hierarchy

Do not overload the user with animations.

Use subtle transitions only:

- Button hover

- Navigation transitions

- Image hover

- Gentle section reveal

Animations should never interfere with usability or page speed.

--------------------------------------------------

SEO

--------------------------------------------------

Implement basic on-page SEO structure.

Each page should have:

- Unique title

- Meta description

- One clear H1

- Logical H2/H3 hierarchy

- Descriptive image alt text

- Semantic HTML

Suggested page titles:

Home:

"[Company Name] | International Trading & Import Export"

About:

"About [Company Name] | International Trading Company"

Products:

"Products | [Company Name] | Import & Export"

Contact:

"Contact [Company Name] | International Trading"

Do not keyword-stuff.

--------------------------------------------------

PERFORMANCE

--------------------------------------------------

Prioritize fast loading.

- Optimize images

- Use modern image formats where possible

- Lazy-load images below the fold

- Avoid unnecessary JavaScript

- Avoid heavy animation libraries

- Avoid unnecessary third-party scripts

- Keep the site lightweight

--------------------------------------------------

ACCESSIBILITY

--------------------------------------------------

Follow basic accessibility practices:

- Proper semantic HTML

- Sufficient color contrast

- Descriptive alt text

- Keyboard-accessible navigation

- Visible focus states

- Accessible form labels

- Proper button/link semantics

--------------------------------------------------

DESIGN DETAILS

--------------------------------------------------

Use:

- Large editorial-style photography

- Clean grid system

- Generous whitespace

- Thin borders

- Subtle shadows

- Restrained corner radius

- Strong typography

- High-quality product photography

Avoid:

- Huge rounded containers

- Excessive cards

- Excessive shadows

- Excessive gradients

- Stock-photo-looking corporate people

- Generic AI illustrations

- Overly decorative icons

- Excessive text

- Excessive animations

The overall impression should be:

"Established international B2B trading company"

rather than:

"AI-generated startup website."

--------------------------------------------------

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

--------------------------------------------------

Build the frontend with a clean, maintainable component structure.

Use reusable components for:

- Header

- Navigation

- Footer

- Buttons

- Product cards

- Section headings

- Contact information

- CTA sections

Keep content separate from reusable UI components where practical so that products and company information can easily be updated later.

Use placeholder data for:

- Company name

- Logo

- Products

- Countries

- Contact information

- Address

- Phone

- Email

- Social links

Clearly mark all placeholders so they can be replaced later.

Do not invent factual company information.

--------------------------------------------------

IMPORTANT FINAL DESIGN RULE

--------------------------------------------------

Before implementing the UI, think like a professional international B2B web designer.

The site should communicate credibility within the first 5–10 seconds.

A foreign buyer should immediately understand:

1. Who we are

2. What we trade

3. Whether we import/export

4. Which markets we serve

5. How to contact us

The website should feel polished, trustworthy, international, and commercially credible.

Do not make it visually complicated simply to make it look impressive.

Simplicity, typography, photography, spacing, and information hierarchy should create the premium appearance.
